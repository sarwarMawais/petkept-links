Petkept — Community guidelines

A household is the few people who look after the same pets: a partner, flatmates, a family, a sitter. These guidelines are short because the space is small, but they apply to everything shared in one: log entries, notes, photos, documents and names.

Please don't share

  • Anything unkind, threatening or hurtful aimed at someone in the household.
  • Sexual content, or violent or graphic images, including of animals being harmed.
  • Anything illegal, or that encourages harming a person or an animal.
  • Other people's private information that they have not agreed to share (addresses, phone numbers, documents about them).
  • Spam, advertising, or content that is not about looking after the pets.

If something is wrong

  • Report it in the app. "Report" is on every other person in Settings → Your household, and on any entry or moment someone else recorded. Choose a reason, add a few words if you like, and send. Reports go to the people who make the app, never to the person you reported.
  • We read every report within 24 hours and act on what breaks these guidelines: we may remove content, remove someone from a household, or end an account.
  • The household's owner can remove someone, and tick "Don't let them join again" so that account cannot come back.
  • Anyone can leave a household at any time and keep a private copy of the record.

If someone is in danger, contact the emergency services first. For anything else, write to mawaissarwar15@gmail.com.
