Deleting your Petkept account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → "Delete my account". (If you never shared a household there is no account; Settings → "Remove everything from this phone" removes your record.)

If you no longer have the app, write to mawaissarwar15@gmail.com from any address, say which household you were in and the name you were shown by, and we will delete the account within 30 days and write back to say so.

What deleting does - Every household membership you have ends. - On what you recorded in a shared household, your name is replaced by "A former member". The entries themselves stay: they are the household's record of its pets, kept for the others. - Invitations you sent that nobody used stop working. - The account is deleted. - In the app, you choose whether to keep a private copy of the record on your phone or remove it.

What is kept, and for how long - Reports you sent, without your account, for up to 12 months. - Our provider's backups, for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them.
