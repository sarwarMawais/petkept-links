Petkept — Privacy policy

Effective: 31 October 2026 Who we are: TubeAI Labs, an independent app developer based in the United Kingdom ("we"). We decide how the data described here is handled (we are the "controller"). Write to us at mawaissarwar15@gmail.com; we answer every message within 24 hours.

The short version

  • Your pets' record lives on your phone. The app works without an account, without a network, and without sending anything anywhere.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you share. If you turn on sharing, your household's record is kept on a server in the European Union so the people you share with see it.
  • Ads in the free version, never from your pets' record. The free version shows small banner ads from Google AdMob, with your consent where the law asks for it (the EU, the UK, Switzerland). Your pets' record is never given to an advertiser. The Household plan removes ads. We add no analytics of our own (Google's ads SDK measures its ads, as below), and we never sell or rent anyone's information.
  • You can take everything with you, and delete your account in the app.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK). Where the law asks, Google's consent form asks you first, and nothing is requested until you have answered; you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy and help → Ad privacy choices. Google's software collects what it needs to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud: an advertising identifier, your approximate location from your internet address, how ads are seen and tapped, and diagnostics. Nothing from your pets' record, your household or your account is passed to it. On the iPhone we do not ask to track you, so every ad there is a non-personalised one. Ads are never shown to a household with the Household plan. Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you put into the app is stored on your phone: your pets and their details (name, kind, birthday, the day they came home, microchip, allergies, photo), the care log (meals, walks, doses and anything else you record, with notes and amounts), schedules and reminders, the health record (vaccinations, vet visits, conditions, weights, lab results, heat cycles), documents you add (PDFs and images), the journal (photos and words), the supplies you keep track of and what you record spending on your pets, and your settings.

This stays on your phone unless you choose to share it (section 2) or to export it (section 5). On Android the app does not take part in the phone's cloud backup, so it is not copied anywhere without you. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no server sends them.

1a. A lost-pet poster page you put online

If one of your pets goes missing, you can put their poster online as a web page anyone with the link can open. Only what you see in the confirmation goes on it: their name, kind and photo, what you typed about how they look and where they were last seen, and the contact you typed. Never their microchip number. Links typed into it are removed. The page is never offered to search engines.

It comes down by itself after 90 days, and you can take it down at any time; either way its contents are deleted from the server, except that "home" keeps the pet's name to say so. Anyone can report the page; reports are kept without any detail about who sent them, and are read within a day. Putting a page online signs your phone in to the server anonymously if it has never shared.

Your phone's own backup. On Android, your pets' record (not your photos or documents) is included in your phone's backup to your Google account, encrypted with your screen lock so that neither Google nor we can read it, and a new phone gets it back when you set it up. You can turn Android's backup off in your phone's settings.

2. If you share a household

Sharing is off until you turn it on. When you start or join a household:

What goes to the server - Your household's record, as in section 1, so everyone in the household sees it: pets, care log, schedules, health record, documents, journal, supplies, and photos and document files. What you spend goes too, and is seen by the household's owners and members only: never by a sitter, whom the server refuses it. - The name you choose to be shown by in the household, and which person did each entry. - An account identifier. Sharing creates an account with no email, no phone number and no password: only a random identifier and the keys that let your phone reach the household. If you choose to keep your account with Sign in with Apple or Google (section 3), the account is also linked to that sign-in.

Where: with our database provider, Supabase (supabase.com), in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Supabase, Inc. is a US company; if any of this data is handled outside the UK and the EEA, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the UK's addendum to them.

Who sees it: the people in your household, and nobody else. Access is enforced by the server, not only by the app: a person removed from a household, or a sitter whose stay has ended, stops receiving the record because the server refuses them.

Invitations are one-time codes that expire; we store only a scrambled form (a hash) of each code, never the code itself.

Sitter pages: if you make a page for a sitter, anyone with its link can see, until it expires or you take it back: your living pets' names and kinds, their current medication and times, active conditions, allergies and microchip numbers. Nothing else, and no names of people. We store only a hash of the link's secret.

2a. The Household plan

The Household plan is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment; we never see card details. So that one purchase covers everyone in your household on Android and iPhone, the purchase is kept by our purchases provider, RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), under your household's identifier: a random code, with no name or email. RevenueCat receives what the store says about the purchase (which plan, when it renews or ends) and processes it on our behalf. RevenueCat, Inc. is a US company; the transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the UK's addendum to them. So that a purchase made on another phone of your household shows on yours, the app asks RevenueCat when it starts whether your household has the plan; that question carries only the household's identifier and basic technical details of the app and phone that RevenueCat's software sends (such as the app's version, the platform and the store's country), never your record. In the stores' privacy answers this identifier is declared as a user ID.

3. Signing in with Apple or Google (optional)

If you choose to keep your account, you sign in with Apple or Google so that a new phone can come back to your household. We receive a token from Apple or Google that says which account it is. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address. Apple and Google handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies.

4. Reports

If you report a person or an entry in your household, we receive the report: what was reported, the reason you chose, any words you added, and which account sent it. The person reported is never told who reported them. We read every report within 24 hours and keep it for 12 months after it is handled. If a household owner blocks someone, we keep that account's identifier so it cannot rejoin that household.

5. Exports and backups you make

Paperwork (for a vet, a sitter, a kennel, an insurer), a pet's record file, the care log as a spreadsheet, and "Export everything" are made on your phone and handed to your phone's share sheet. We do not receive them; where they go is up to you. A backup ("Export everything") contains your household's whole record and the names of the people in it, so that restoring it says who did what; please keep it somewhere you trust. A pet's record file, made to pass a pet on to a new home, contains no one's name.

6. Why we use this data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for, including sharing when you turn it on: performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the household safe and answer reports: our legitimate interest in running a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f)), and the app stores' rules.
  • To comply with the law where it requires us to keep or disclose something (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Information about a pet's health is not "health data" about a person. If you write something about a person's health in a note, it is stored like any other note, and only for the purpose of your record.

7. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you remove it, remove everything from the phone (Settings), or uninstall.
  • On the server: while your household exists. What you recorded in a shared household is part of that household's record and stays with it for the others, under your name, after you leave, unless you delete your account (section 8), when your name is replaced by "A former member".
  • Backups kept by our provider: up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Leave a household (Settings → Your household): keep a private copy on your phone, or remove it from your phone.
  • Delete your account (Settings): every membership you have ends, your name is replaced by "A former member" on what you recorded, invitations you sent that nobody used stop working, and the account is deleted. You choose whether to keep a copy of the record on your phone. A public page explains this without needing the app: https://sarwarmawais.github.io/petkept-links/delete-account.html.
  • Remove everything from this phone (Settings), if you never shared.
  • Take your data with you: "Export everything" (Settings) gives you all of it in one file.
  • Your GDPR rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability. Most you can exercise in the app; for anything else write to mawaissarwar15@gmail.com. You may also complain to a supervisory authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the data protection authority where you live.

9. Children

The app is for adults who care for pets. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, or under a higher age where the law where you live sets one.

10. Security

Data travels to the server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The server allows each account to read only the households it belongs to. Your phone keeps your record in the app's private storage.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
