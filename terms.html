Effective: 25 October 2026. These terms are between you and TubeAI Labs, an independent app developer based in the United Kingdom.

1. What the app is, and is not

Petkept keeps a record of the care you and your household give your pets. It is not a medical device and does not give veterinary or medical advice, say what is wrong with a pet, or recommend a dose. Amounts and doses you enter are recorded as you wrote them; the app never calculates one. Reminders help you remember; your phone may delay or silence a notification, so the record in the app is the one to trust. If you are ever worried about a pet's health, talk to your vet.

2. Your account and your household
